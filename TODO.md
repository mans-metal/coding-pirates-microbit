# To check in real MakeCode

These were written from documentation and memory. Check them in the editor (Settings, Language, Dansk for the Danish ones) and fix the pages and `tools/blocks.py` if they differ.

- Danish block words marked as guesses in `tools/blocks.py`: `hvis`, `så`, `ellers` (if, then, else), and the Danish for `and`.
- Blocks with no Danish text in MakeCode's translation feed, so the editor shows them in English: `rest`, `sprite is touching`, `delete sprite`.
- `ændr ... af` (change variable): the Danish feed says "af", which may be a translation slip. Pictures follow the feed.
- Where each block lives: `clear screen` and `set pull` under "... more", pins blocks under Advanced, Game blocks, `set built-in speaker` (V2 only).
- The block `play tone`: newer editors may show "play tone ... until done".
- Sound on a V1 comes out of pin 0, and on a V2 from the speaker and pin 0. The servo on pin 1 on USB power or the battery pack.
- What each kit sensor (PIR, crash sensor) reads, and the PIR settle time.
- That the Tinkercademy kit blocks are not needed for the lessons (the pages use the core pin blocks).
- Pin facts for a V2 (pin 9 free, pin 12 reserved), from tech.microbit.org.
